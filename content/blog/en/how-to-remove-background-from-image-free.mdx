- **Passport and ID photos** — many countries require a white background

## Tips for the Best Background Removal Results

The quality of background removal depends heavily on the original image. Here is how to get clean results:

**High contrast between subject and background**
The tool works best when the subject and background are clearly different in color or tone. A person in a red jacket against a white wall = perfect. A person in a beige shirt against a sand background = harder.

**Sharp edges and good lighting**
Well-lit photos with sharp focus on the subject produce cleaner cutouts. Avoid blurry or low-resolution images.

**Simple backgrounds remove more cleanly**
Solid colors, gradients, or uniform textures (like a studio backdrop) are removed more accurately than complex busy backgrounds like crowds or forests.

**Product photos on white work best**
E-commerce product photos shot on a white or light-colored background are ideal for this tool and produce near-perfect results.

**Can I remove the background from a logo?**
Yes. Upload your logo image and the background will be removed, leaving a transparent PNG of just the logo. This is ideal for using logos on different colored backgrounds or in documents.

**Does it work on photos with hair or fur?**
The tool handles most cases well, including portraits with hair. Very fine or wispy hair against a complex background may have minor imperfections at the edges, which is normal for any automated tool.

**Can I process multiple images at once?**
Currently the tool processes one image at a time. Process each image separately — each takes only a few seconds.

**What is a transparent background (PNG)?**
A transparent background means the area around your subject has no color — it's empty. When you place a transparent PNG on a colored background in any application, the empty area shows the underlying color instead of white or black.
