## How background removal works

Modern background removal uses **AI (machine learning)**. A model has been trained on millions of images to tell the difference between a main subject (person, product, animal) and the background. When you upload a photo, the tool runs this model in your browser or on a server: it predicts which pixels belong to the subject and which to the background, then makes the background transparent (or replaces it). The result is usually much cleaner than manual selection, especially around hair, fur, and fine details. Because the work is done by AI, you don’t need to trace edges yourself—you just upload and download.

## Tips for best results

- **Use a clear subject** — Photos with a single, well-defined subject (person, product, object) work best. Busy scenes or many overlapping subjects can confuse the AI.
- **Good contrast** — A subject that stands out from the background (e.g. person against a plain wall) gives cleaner edges than similar colors throughout.
- **Resolution** — Higher resolution input often gives finer edges. If the image is very large, the tool may resize for speed; for print or large display, start with a high-res source.
- **Check the preview** — Before downloading, zoom in on hair, glasses, or thin parts. If something was missed or cut off, try a different crop or a clearer photo.
