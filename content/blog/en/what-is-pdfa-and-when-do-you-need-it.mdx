PDF/A is the ISO 19005 standard for long-term archiving of PDF documents. It is required for official submissions to public administrations, legal filings, and any document that must remain readable and unaltered for decades.

## PDF/A vs regular PDF — key differences

| Feature | Regular PDF | PDF/A |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Font embedding | Optional | Mandatory |
| Encryption | Allowed | Not allowed |
| JavaScript | Allowed | Not allowed |
| External references | Allowed | Not allowed |
| Color profiles | Optional | Required |
| XMP metadata | Optional | Mandatory |
| Long-term readability | Not guaranteed | Guaranteed |

## When is PDF/A required?

- **Public administration (PA)** — submissions to municipalities, cadastre, land registry, building permits
- **Legal filings** — court submissions in Italy and the EU
- **Certified email (PEC)** — attachments for official digital correspondence
- **Building permits and SCIA** — technical documentation for construction projects
- **Medical records** — long-term patient document storage
- **Banking and insurance** — contract archiving

## PDF/A versions explained

- **PDF/A-1b** — basic visual conformance (most common, accepted everywhere)
- **PDF/A-1a** — full conformance including tagged structure
- **PDF/A-2b** — based on PDF 1.7, supports transparency and JPEG 2000
- **PDF/A-3b** — allows embedding of any file type as attachment

For most use cases, **PDF/A-1b** is sufficient.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is PDF/A used for?**
PDF/A is used for long-term archiving of documents that must remain readable and legally valid over time, independent of future software versions.

**Is PDF/A required in Italy?**
Yes. The Italian Digital Administration Code (CAD) requires PDF/A for official document submissions to public administrations.

**What is the difference between PDF/A-1b and PDF/A-2b?**
PDF/A-1b is based on PDF 1.4 and is the most widely accepted version. PDF/A-2b is based on PDF 1.7 and supports transparency layers and JPEG 2000 compression.

**Can I convert a scanned PDF to PDF/A?**
Yes, as long as the PDF is not encrypted. Scanned PDFs (image-only) can be converted to PDF/A — they will be visually conformant even without searchable text.

**Is PDF/A the same as PDF/X?**
No. PDF/X is a standard for print production. PDF/A is for archiving. They serve different purposes.

**How do I know if my PDF is already PDF/A?**
Open it in Adobe Acrobat or validate it with veraPDF. FileFlip also adds a PDF/A identifier to the document metadata that PDF readers can detect.
