# How to Convert Image to Text Free — OCR Online

When you have a photo of a document, a screenshot of an article, or a scanned page, you cannot copy or edit the text — it is locked inside the image. OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology solves this by reading the text in an image and converting it into editable, copyable text. This guide shows you how to do it for free.

## What Is OCR and How Does It Work?

OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It is the technology that reads text inside images by analyzing the shape of letters and words. Modern OCR uses machine learning to recognize text across different fonts, sizes, and languages, even in photos taken at an angle or with imperfect lighting.

OCR converts:
- **Scanned documents** → editable text
- **Screenshots** → copyable text
- **Photos of signs or books** → searchable text
- **Business cards** → contact information
- **Receipts and invoices** → data you can paste into a spreadsheet

## Tips for Better OCR Results

OCR accuracy depends heavily on the image quality. Follow these tips to get the best results:

**Use high-resolution images**
Images below 150 DPI often produce poor OCR results. Scan documents at 300 DPI or higher. If photographing with a phone, make sure the image is sharp and in focus.

**Ensure good contrast**
Dark text on a light background works best. Avoid images where the text color is similar to the background.

**Keep the image flat and straight**
Text on a curved page (like an open book) or taken at an angle is harder to read. Flatten the page and photograph it from directly above.

**Avoid shadows and glare**
Shadows and glare from phone flashes reduce accuracy significantly. Use natural light or a flat lamp for document photos.

**Use PNG instead of JPG for screenshots**
Screenshots saved as PNG are sharper than JPG because PNG is lossless. Upload PNG screenshots for the most accurate text extraction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is OCR?**
OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It is a technology that reads text characters in images and converts them into machine-readable, editable text. Modern OCR uses deep learning to handle different fonts, languages, orientations and image qualities with high accuracy.

**How accurate is image to text conversion?**
Accuracy depends on image quality. For clean, high-resolution scans or screenshots, accuracy is typically above 98%. For low-resolution, blurry or handwritten images, accuracy can be lower. Handwritten text is significantly harder to read than printed text.

**Can OCR read handwritten text?**
Modern OCR can read clear, neatly handwritten text with reasonable accuracy. Cursive handwriting is much harder to read and may produce errors. For best results with handwriting, use high contrast (black ink on white paper) and write in block letters.

**What languages does the OCR support?**
FileFlip's OCR supports the major world languages including English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Arabic and Hindi, among others.

**Can I extract text from a screenshot?**
